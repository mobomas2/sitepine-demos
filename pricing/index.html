Questions

Pricing FAQ

Is it really one-time? No monthly fees at all?

Yes. You pay once — $88 or $189 — and the website is yours. There is no SitePine subscription. Your only recurring cost is your domain name (about $10–15 a year, registered in your own name).

What do I need to provide?

Just your Instagram page. We take your photos, services and style from there. If you have a logo or extra photos, send them too — but they are optional.

How do I pay?

By card or USDT (crypto). A deposit starts the final launch; the balance is due when your site goes live on your domain.

Can I update the site later?

Yes. Small text and photo updates are quick to request, and bigger redesigns are quoted flat — no hourly billing surprises.

What if I don't like the first version?

Then you don't pay. The first version is free to look at — that is the point of building before you pay.
