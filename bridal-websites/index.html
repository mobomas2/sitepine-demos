Bridal website FAQ

How much does a bridal boutique website cost?

With SitePine, from $189 one-time for a full boutique site with collection galleries. Bridal web specialists typically charge $800–$4,000 plus monthly fees — and most publish no price at all, so you only learn the cost after a sales call.

Can brides book a fitting from the website?

Yes. Every collection and dress page can carry a WhatsApp button, so a bride books her fitting in one tap — no forms lost in an inbox, no new system for your team.

My boutique is on Instagram. Is that enough?

Instagram is where brides discover you, but they plan on Google and Pinterest too — and they Google your boutique's name before visiting. A website you own catches that search and turns it into booked fittings.

Which languages can the site be in?

English, Arabic, Russian, Azerbaijani and more. The Wavy Atelier demo below was built for a Baku boutique; Dubai boutiques usually choose English + Arabic.
